Athlete management system
version 1.0
specification:

1) Athlete Profiles: Stores basic personal info, emergency contacts, and history.
2) Workload Tracker: Logs training data, workouts, GPS stats, and session effort.
3) Medical Logs: Tracks injuries, rehab progress, and official play availability
4) Wellness Monitoring: Gathers daily survey data on sleep, fatigue, and soreness.
5) Team Communication: Handles direct messaging, group alerts, and shared calendars.
6) Central Database: Merges data from separate spreadsheets into one profile per athlete.
7) Device Integration: Connects directly with fitness trackers and heart rate monitors
8) Data Security: Restricts sensitive medical records to authorized health staff only
9) Smart Analytics: Flags fatigue trends automatically to prevent player overtraining
